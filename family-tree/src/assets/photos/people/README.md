# Person photos

Name each photo after the person's key in `src/data/people.json`, for example:

- `ernesto.jpg`, `jovita.jpg`, `lourdes.jpg`, `fernando.jpg`, `marietta.jpg`
- `ronaldo.jpg`, `arnold.jpg`, `rebecca.jpg`, `ernestoJr.jpg`, `jeffrey.jpg`

Any of .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp or .avif works, and upper/lower case doesn't matter.
The photo shows on the person's card, their page and the parents page. Square, face-centred
photos look best because they are cropped to a circle. Keep each under about 500 KB.
