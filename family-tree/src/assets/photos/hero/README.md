# Hero photos

Drop up to 9 family photos here. They fill the ring around the hero headline in file-name order,
clockwise from 12 o'clock, so name them `01.jpg`, `02.jpg` … `09.jpg` to control the order.
Slots without a photo keep their grey placeholder. Photos are shown in black and white.

Slot shapes, in order: 1 landscape, 2 portrait (small), 3 portrait, 4 landscape, 5 square,
6 portrait, 7 square (small), 8 portrait, 9 landscape. Photos are cropped to fit.
